💡 Transfer Learning Impact

Fine-tuning the deeper convolutional layers (Layer 4) reduced the error rate from 21.2% down to 8.89%, demonstrating how specialized microscopic feature representations adapt beyond generic ImageNet weights.

🔬 Clinical Conidia Morphology

Microsporum species (H5 & H6) achieved near-perfect recognition (97%–99% F1) due to distinctive echinulate macroconidia walls, while distinguishing H1 from H2 required deeper representations due to subtle hyphal variations.
